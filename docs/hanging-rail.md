# Hanging rail: set it up

The Hanging rail section shows your clothes as they would hang in a shop: a
metal rail across the page and each garment on its hanger, with a price tag.
When a visitor points at a garment it turns to face them and its neighbours
move aside. Clicking it opens a focus view with the garment large, its sizes
and Add to cart. On a phone the row swipes and a tap opens the same view as a
sheet.

Every template has it. In the website builder, choose Add section and look for
**Hanging rail of products**.

## 1. Photograph each garment on a hanger

You need one photo per garment, on its hanger, from the front. A side view and
a back view are optional.

- Hang the garment on a plain wall or a door, in even daylight, with nothing
  overlapping it.
- Stand square to it and keep the whole hanger, hook included, in the frame.
- Use the same kind of hanger for every garment if you can. The rail looks
  calmer when the hangers match.
- For a side view, turn the hanger a quarter turn and take a second photo. If
  you skip it, the section turns the front view to look like one.

Product photos you already have work too, as long as the garment is on a
hanger and nothing covers it.

## 2. Cut out the background

The pictures must be **transparent PNG or WebP cut-outs**: the garment and its
hanger, with nothing behind them.

1. Open the photo in any background remover (most photo apps and phone gallery
   apps have one, and there are free websites that do it in one click).
2. Check the edges: white garments on white walls and the thin wire of the
   hook are the parts that go wrong most. Paint back anything it removed.
3. Crop close to the garment, with the **hook at the top centre** of the
   picture. The section hangs every picture from the middle of its top edge.
4. Save it as PNG or WebP with transparency (not JPEG, which has no
   transparency).

Recommended sizes:

| Picture | Size | Shown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Front view | about 1000 x 1300 px | on hover and in the focus view |
| Side view | about 400 x 1000 px | on the rail |
| Back view | about 1000 x 1300 px | only when a visitor presses Back |

## 3. Add the pictures and link the products

1. Add a **Hanging rail** section to your page.
2. Add one **Garment** block for each piece on the rail.
3. In each block, choose its **Product**. The title, price, sizes and product
   page come from the product, so they stay right when you change a price.
4. Upload the **Front view** and, if you have them, the **Side view** and
   **Back view**.
5. **Price tag** is optional: leave it empty to show the price, or write
   something of your own, such as "New".
6. **Note in the focus view** is one short line, such as the fabric.

Drag the blocks to change the order of the rail.

## 4. Settings worth changing

- **Garment height** and **Garment height on phones**: how big the garments
  hang.
- **Room for a garment on the rail**: how much width each garment gets. Narrow
  side views suit 30 to 40%.
- **Gap between garments** and **Hover side gap**: how close they hang, and
  how much room the neighbours leave a garment turned to the front.
- **Hover turn speed**: how quickly a garment turns.
- **Hanger swing**: how much hangers swing as the pointer brushes past. Set it
  to 0 for none. Visitors who ask their device for less motion never see it.
- **When the garments are wider than the screen**: let the row scroll (drag,
  swipe or arrows), or make every garment smaller so all fit.
- **Clicking a garment**: open the focus view, or go straight to the product
  page.
- **Background**: a plain colour, or a picture (a landscape works well) with a
  shade over it. Keep the middle of the picture quiet so the garments read.
- **Under the rail**: centred name and button, or split with the name on the
  left and your line and link on the right.

## Checking it

- Point at each garment: it should turn to the front with its hook on the rail.
  A garment that floats or sits low has empty space above its hook: crop it
  again.
- Open the focus view on a phone and add a size to the cart.
- If a hanger shows a grey or white halo on a dark background, the cut-out
  kept some of the wall: clean the edge in the background remover.
