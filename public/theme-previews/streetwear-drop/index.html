Questions, answered

Boxy: wide in the shoulder and a little short in the body. Take your usual size for the intended fit, or one down for a closer one. Every product page has the measurements.

Orders placed before 2 pm ship the same working day. You get a tracking link by email as soon as it leaves.

Write to us within an hour of ordering and we will change the size or cancel it. After that it is usually packed.

Send anything unworn back within 30 days for a refund or another size. The returns page has the address and a printable label.

Cold, inside out, and never in the dryer. Dry hoodies flat and tees on a wide hanger so they keep their length.
