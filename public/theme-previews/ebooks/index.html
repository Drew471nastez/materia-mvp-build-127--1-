Why we write them

Food that keeps you going, without the fuss

“Our grandparents ate broth, liver, butter and slow bread, and nobody called it a diet. We wanted those recipes back on a weeknight table: tested in a home kitchen, with quantities you can shop for and steps that fit around work.”

Our kitchen

What you will cook

  • Broths and stocks

    Beef, chicken, fish and bone broth, and forty soups and sauces that start with a pot of it.

  • The whole animal

    Liver, heart, marrow, tongue and the cheaper cuts, cooked so the whole table eats them.

  • Ferments

    Kraut, kimchi, kvass, pickles, yoghurt and cultured butter, with a week by week plan.

  • Meal plans

    Four weeks of dinners with shopping lists and one batch cooking afternoon a week.
