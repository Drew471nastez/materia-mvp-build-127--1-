Online coaching

Questions

About coaching, the programs and the free call.

Do I need a gym?

No. Every program has a gym version and a home version for dumbbells and a bench. Online coaching works with whatever you have.

I have never lifted. Is that a problem?

Most people I coach started there. The first weeks teach the lifts with light weights and plenty of video feedback.

What happens on the free call?

Twenty minutes on video. We talk about your training, your week and what you want. If I am not the right coach for you, I say so and point you somewhere better.

Can I pause online coaching?

Yes. Pause or stop before your next month starts, by email or from your account. There is no minimum term.

Do you write meal plans?

I give simple guidance on protein, meals around training and sleep. For a full meal plan or a medical condition I refer you to a registered dietitian.

What if I get injured?

Tell me in your log. We train around it, and I point you to a physio when it needs one. I am a coach, not a doctor.

Still deciding?

Book the free call or send me a message. I answer every message myself, within a day.
