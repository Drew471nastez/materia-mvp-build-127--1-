Help and questions

Before you order

Sizes, delivery, returns and the prints.

  • Which size should I take?

    Tees and crews are cut a little boxy with a dropped shoulder. If you are between two sizes, take the smaller one for a closer fit. Every product page has the measurements.

  • When will my order arrive?

    Each piece is printed when you order it and leaves within two working days, tracked. Add your own delivery times on the shipping page.

  • Can I send something back?

    Yes, within 30 days if it is unworn and unwashed. Write to us with your order number and we send a return label. See shipping and returns.

  • Will the print crack or fade?

    The prints are water based ink pressed into the cotton, so they stay soft. Wash cold and inside out, hang to dry, and they last as long as the tee.
