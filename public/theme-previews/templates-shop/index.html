Questions people ask first

Still unsure? Write to me and I will answer within a day or two.

Do I need a paid Notion plan?

No. Every template works on Notion's free plan. You only need an account.

How do I get the template after I pay?

Your receipt has a link to the template. Open it while signed in to Notion and press Duplicate. It is in your workspace a few seconds later.

Can I change it?

Yes. Once you duplicate it, it is your page. Rename columns, add properties, change the colours, delete what you do not use.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works anywhere Notion does: the Mac and Windows apps, the browser, and the iPhone and Android apps.

Can I share it with my team?

You can share your copy inside your own workspace. Please do not resell it or post the duplicate link publicly.

What if it is not right for me?

Write within 14 days and you get your money back. No form, just reply to your receipt.
