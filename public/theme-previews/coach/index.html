Hi, I'm Amara

I coach people who run before work

I ran the 400 metres in college, then spent years getting injured doing too much, too fast. These days I coach nurses, teachers, parents and night shift workers who want to run a race and still feel good on Monday.

Most of my runners run less than they expect and finish stronger than they hoped.

Monthly coaching

How coaching works

Four weeks at a time, built around the week you actually have.

  1. 1

    01Tell me about your running

    A short form: your recent weeks, your race, your job and how many days you can run.

  2. 2

    02Get four weeks of runs

    Every session with a time, an effort and a reason. Synced to your phone's calendar.

  3. 3

    03Run, log it, hear back

    Log each run with a line on how it felt. I read them all and reply within a day.

  4. 4

    04Race week

    A taper, a pacing plan for the course and a call two days before you run.
