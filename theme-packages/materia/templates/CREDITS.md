Round 26: Storelib Default's About and sign up pictures have a tablet's maker mark and a mug's printed words painted out; the Notion shop's photographs of its maker (one session, one person) have the laptop's maker mark painted out; OKTAV's About pictures are turned black and white and the sewing machine's name is painted out. The Course template's hero has the badges on its two speakers painted out. The Art books hero's left picture is Paul Cézanne's Still Life with a Ginger Jar and Eggplants, which The Metropolitan Museum of Art shares as public domain.
The Art books template's book covers, open book spreads and tablet are drawn around public domain paintings, prints and photographs that The Metropolitan Museum of Art shares on Unsplash; their rows name each source.

The Notion templates' and the Digital products shop's device pictures are Unsplash photographs of laptops, tablets and a phone with a screen drawn for the template set into the display in perspective; their rows name the photograph. The Digital products bundle screen shows a small crop of https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1693328787539-1477754c01b7 (Unsplash License).
The Creator Landing's photographs are one session by one photographer (Jonathan Borba): the same artist at her desk, used as the creator, Ines Hart.
The Cookbook template's (ebooks folder, round 22) book covers and hardback pictures are drawn for it around Unsplash food photographs, which their rows name; its kitchen, table and pasture pictures are Unsplash photographs.

Rows marked "cover only" are sample product pictures for the listing covers (scripts/theme-templates/shops.ts).

Every garment on the rail is a real Unsplash photograph of it on its hanger, cut out by machine (rembg, BiRefNet and IS-Net models) and cleaned by hand. One hanger for all: each photograph's own hanger is toned to the same natural wood, the white hoodie's plastic hanger recoloured as wood, and the charcoal long sleeve, whose hook was out of frame, given a drawn steel hook. The grey, oat, forest and charcoal hoodies are the white hoodie photograph tinted; they are not photographs of those colours. The side views on the rail (assets/rail-*-side.webp, round 25) are drawn for this template from each garment's own front cut-out: the sleeve, body and hood fabric are strips of that photograph resampled into a garment hanging side on, then shaded; they are not photographs of the garments from the side. Round 28: every sample product's first picture is now a real photograph of someone wearing that kind of piece in that colour (the photographs are of other people's clothes, chosen for the cut and colour), and its second is the garment alone: the hanger cut-out the rail hangs, the same transparent file as the rail's front view, so the focus view shows it once.
