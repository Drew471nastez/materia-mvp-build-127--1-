Copy is half of whether a page reads as made by a person.

**Do**
- Name the thing that happens: "Twelve week strength block, three sessions a week" beats "Transform your fitness journey".
- Use the creator's own words from their bio, products and posts. Their spelling, their jokes.
- Headlines under about eight words. Body lines that say one thing.
- Buttons as verbs with objects: "Book a session", "Get the sample pack", "Read the menu".
- Numbers only when they are real and come from the store.

**Don't**
- The words that mark machine copy: unlock, elevate, seamless, powerful, effortless(ly), supercharge, transform, empower, streamline, revolutionise, next level, game changer, cutting edge, world class, unleash, journey (as a metaphor), curated (unless it is literally a curation).
- Formulas: "X meets Y", "Not just A. B.", "Where A meets B", three adjective triplets ("Bold. Simple. Yours."), rhetorical questions as headlines.
- Em dashes and decorative hyphens. Use a full stop or a comma.
- Fake urgency: "Only 3 left!" without stock data, countdowns to nothing.

**Rewrite examples**
- "Elevate your sound with our powerful sample packs" -> "Drum breaks and textures recorded on tape in Glasgow".
- "Unlock your potential with seamless coaching" -> "One to one coaching for runners coming back from injury".
