The fastest route to a generic site is to take the industry default: every fitness coach gets black, neon green and a condensed sans. Instead, place each concept on five axes and deliberately choose a position.

| Axis | One end | Other end |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tone | quiet, considered | loud, declarative |
| Era | classic, heritage | contemporary, futurist |
| Density | airy, few elements | dense, information rich |
| Temperature | warm neutrals, earth | cool, mineral, high contrast |
| Type voice | serif, literary | grotesk, condensed, mono |

Two fitness coaches, same brief:
- **"Quiet Strength"**: quiet, classic, airy, warm, serif. Cream background, deep olive text, a high contrast serif display, photos of slow movement in natural light, no motion beyond fades.
- **"Training Log"**: loud, contemporary, dense, cool, mono. Off white with black rules, a monospace for numbers and schedules, a ticker of session times, hard cut image transitions.

Rules: pick at least two axes far from the category default. Use the creator's own material (their photos, their way of talking) to decide which. When a previous concept exists for the same creator or a sibling site, `suggestDirections` in concept.ts avoids its positions. Never vary randomly; every position needs a reason drawn from the brand.
