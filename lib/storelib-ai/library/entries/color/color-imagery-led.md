When a creator has strong imagery (album covers, product photography, a signature wardrobe), the palette should come from it, not from a preset.

**Method**: pick the dominant dark (becomes text or dark surface), the dominant light (background), and one saturated colour that appears in small amounts (accent). Ignore colours that appear in only one image.

**Per collection or release**: let a collection page or a release page take its accent from its own cover, while the site frame stays neutral. Pass the accent as a section colour setting so the creator can change it.

**Check**: the derived accent still needs a contrast safe text colour on top; derived greys still need 4.5:1 for text.

**Do not** sample colours that only exist because of a bad white balance, or a stock photo's colours. Real brand material only.
