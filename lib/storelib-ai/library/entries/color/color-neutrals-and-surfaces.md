- **Temperature**: tint neutrals toward the brand. Warm brands use greys with a little yellow or red (`#f4f1ec`, `#e7e2da`, `#5f5a52`); cool brands use blue grey (`#eef1f4`, `#d9dee4`, `#56606b`). Pure `#f5f5f5` greys look default.
- **Surface steps**: background, one surface step (about 3 to 5% darker or lighter), one border. Three levels are enough for almost any site.
- **Borders**: 1px at about 1.3:1 to 1.6:1 against the background for structure lines; stronger (3:1) for input boundaries.
- **Separation without cards**: whitespace, a hairline rule, a change in background between sections, alignment. Use a card only when the item is a distinct, clickable object (a product, a post, a plan).
- **Shadows**: if used, one soft elevation (`0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,.06), 0 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,.05)`) for raised overlays like menus and dialogs, not for every block.
