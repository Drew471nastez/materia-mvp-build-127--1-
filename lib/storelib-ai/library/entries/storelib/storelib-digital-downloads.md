**Show before buying**: real previews (audio clips, page spreads, screenshots, before and after for presets), the contents list (counts, formats, file size, compatibility) from product fields or the creator's description, and the licence in plain words.

**Delivery line**: "Download right after checkout. We also email you the link." Only if that is how the product is set up (Storelib digital products deliver files after purchase).

**Variants**: different licences or formats as variants with real prices; explain the difference in a short table.

**Buy**: one item purchase goes straight to `checkout_url`; bundles are their own products.

**Never**: invent file counts, BPM ranges, "used by top producers", or download numbers.
