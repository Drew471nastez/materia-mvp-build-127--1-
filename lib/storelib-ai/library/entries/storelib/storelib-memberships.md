**Show**: the membership's name, real price and billing period from the product, what members get with frequency ("a new sample pack every month", "two live Q&As a month"), what unlocks immediately after joining (community access, archive, discounts), and how to cancel if the creator offers cancel any time.

**Tiers**: only the tiers that exist as products. Compare them by differences, not by repeating shared benefits in each column.

**Proof**: real member quotes if supplied. Never "Join 5,000 members" without data.

**Join flow**: the action goes to the membership's real checkout. After joining, link people to where the benefit lives (the community, the downloads page).

**Avoid**: "Exclusive", "VIP", "inner circle" language without concrete benefits; countdowns to price rises that are not scheduled.
