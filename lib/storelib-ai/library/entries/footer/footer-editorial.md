**Contents**: a short line about the publication or brand, sections list, credits (design, photography with licences), newsletter, social.

**Layout**: an asymmetric grid: statement across 6 columns, link groups in narrow columns, rules between rows.

**Type**: the statement in the display or reading serif at 20 to 28px; lists in small caps.

**Good detail**: photo credits for open licence images gathered here or on a credits page (Storelib's AI builder makes a photo credits page for Openverse images).
