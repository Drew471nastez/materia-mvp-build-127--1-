**Source**: only profiles the creator has (`shop.socials`); never placeholder links to instagram.com.

**Format**: text links ("Instagram", "YouTube") are clearer than icons for most audiences; icons need `aria-label` and 24px glyphs in 44px hit areas.

**Order**: by where the audience actually is (a music producer: SoundCloud, YouTube, Instagram).

**Creator sites**: social can be primary content (latest video embed, recent posts) rather than just icons.
