**When**: a focused landing page, a checkout step, a full screen experience, a link in bio page.

**Still required**: legal links where the store sells (privacy, terms, refunds) reachable from the page (a small line at the end works); contact for support; the copyright line is optional.

**Never**: remove policies from a page that takes payment.

**Replacement pattern**: end the page with the primary action and a single quiet line underneath: brand name, a contact email, and the policy links, in 13 to 14px muted text. That keeps the page focused while still meeting what a selling site owes its visitors.
