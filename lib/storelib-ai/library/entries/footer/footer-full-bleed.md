**When**: brand led sites where the footer is the last impression: venues, studios, fashion.

**Shape**: full width background (dark scheme or a photo with overlay), the closing call to action or contact large, essentials (address, hours, links) below in small type.

**Contrast**: text on photo needs an overlay; check the brightest area.

**Rhythm**: if the section above is already dark and full bleed, use a lighter footer to separate them.
