**Visitor**: another musician deciding in seconds whether the sound fits their work. Hearing is the proof; reading is secondary.

**Structure for a pack page**: pack name and cover art -> a play button that works without scrolling -> what is inside (counts, formats, BPM range, key labels, file size) from the product's own fields -> a demo track and a few individual previews -> licence in one plain paragraph (royalty free? credit needed?) -> price and buy -> other packs.

**Home**: latest release or pack front and centre, a strip of covers, credits (artists worked with) only if real, services (mixing, custom beats) with how to enquire.

**Visual directions**: cover art drives the palette; pull the accent from the current release. Options: *tape lab* (warm off white, monospace specs, waveform as a thin line, analogue grain); *night bus* (near black, one saturated accent, condensed display); *gallery* (white, big square covers in a strict grid, tiny captions). Avoid generic equaliser bar animations and purple neon by default.

**Audio rules**: never autoplay with sound. One player at a time (starting one pauses the others). Show duration and a scrubbable progress bar, keyboard operable. Previews come from the product's files, never placeholder audio.

**Storelib**: packs are digital products with downloads delivered after checkout; beats with different licences are variants with real prices.
