**Visitor**: a couple deciding in a few minutes whether the photographer feels right, then checking dates and packages.

**Style**: light editorial with portfolio pacing. Seed: Wedding Light.

**Layout moves**: stories as sequences of frames in varied widths, each with one line; a packages table from real prices; availability by month if the creator supplies it; an enquiry form that asks for date and place; a short personal note in the photographer's voice.

**Type**: Italiana or Instrument Serif display with Jost or Instrument Sans text.

**Motion**: subtle: images reveal as they enter, nothing bounces.

**Imagery**: candid, backlit, film colour. **Avoid**: watermark heavy galleries, invented magazine features, star ratings.
