**Principle**: luxury is the absence of pressure. Fewer elements, more space, slower pace, exact details.

**Structure**: a single image or film with the brand name and nothing else -> a collection or the craft story told in two or three spreads -> products from the store with large imagery, name and price in small type -> the atelier or maker -> appointments or contact (a private viewing is a booking) -> discreet newsletter.

**Type**: a refined serif or a thin, wide sans for display, set with generous tracking in capitals for small labels (0.1em to 0.2em), sentence case for body. Small body sizes (15 to 16px) with large leading are fine on desktop; never below 16px on phones.

**Colour**: ivory, bone, charcoal, black, or one deep brand colour (bottle green, oxblood, navy). Gold only as a hairline or in photography, never as a gradient fill.

**Motion**: slow (600 to 900ms), eased out, image reveals with a mask, nothing bouncy.

**Never**: discount banners, "SALE", countdowns, badges, emoji, review stars, pill buttons, more than one call to action per screen. Prices come from the store; do not hide them unless the creator asked.
