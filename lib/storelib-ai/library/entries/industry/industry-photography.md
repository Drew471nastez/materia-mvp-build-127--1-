**Rule one**: never crop someone else's composition into a square card. Show photos at their own ratio, or in a grid whose row heights adapt (justified rows) so nothing is cut.

**Structure**: one strong image or a short sequence as the opening (no text over faces) -> galleries by type (weddings, portraits, commercial) -> about with a portrait of the photographer -> packages with real prices or "from" prices the creator set -> enquiry or booking form with date, location, type -> prints shop if products exist.

**Gallery**: lightbox with keyboard arrows, Escape to close, swipe on touch, focus returned to the thumbnail; captions optional. Lazy load everything after the first row. Neutral surroundings: near white or near black, thin type, no drop shadows or rounded corners on photos.

**Directions**: a gallery wall (white, generous margins, tiny sans captions); a darkroom (charcoal, warm grey type, images glow by contrast only); a zine (mixed sizes, handwritten-like display used once, tape or paper edges sparingly).

**Don't**: watermarks across the whole image, auto playing slideshows with dissolves every 3 seconds, music, right click blockers.
