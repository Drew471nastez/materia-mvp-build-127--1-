**Visitor**: imagining an object in their own room: size, material, care, delivery, price.

**Style**: contemporary ecommerce with editorial pacing; warm greys, oak, linen; an accent from a signature material. Seed: Showroom Floor.

**Layout moves**: a product grid with uneven sizes (one large, several small) instead of equal tiles; room scenes between product rows; a materials library (wood, finish, fabric) as a horizontal gallery; dimensions as a small line drawing or a table; a hover image reveal list for collections.

**Type**: Familjen Grotesk or Hanken Grotesk display, Karla text; numbers tabular for dimensions and prices.

**Motion**: subtle: a second image on hover, a sticky product summary while the details scroll.

**Imagery**: real rooms, daylight, material close ups, the workshop. **Avoid**: white cut outs only, 3D renders as photos, invented lead times, discount banners that are not real.
