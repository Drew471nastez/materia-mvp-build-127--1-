**Visitor**: buying a gift by a deadline, or a plant for a particular room.

**Style**: organic and gently playful. Seed: Green Room.

**Layout moves**: care cards (light, water, pet safety) for every plant; a seasonal list of what is in; delivery area and order cut off time near the buy button; plant size shown against a hand or a chair; the site's own cart drawer with a gift note.

**Type**: Fraunces with Lexend text, or Instrument Serif with Instrument Sans for a florist studio.

**Motion**: subtle; petals do not fall.

**Imagery**: plants in real homes, stems and roots, the shop's own arrangements. **Avoid**: green gradients, generic leaf icons beside every heading, invented same day promises.
