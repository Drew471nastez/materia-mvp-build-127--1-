**Visitor**: a fan looking for the new release, tour dates or merch.

**Style**: typography led and industrial, or dark cinematic for electronic labels. Seeds: Pressing Plant, Night Shift.

**Layout moves**: a horizontal scroll crate of releases with catalogue numbers; a release page with tracklist in mono, formats and real prices; tour dates as a dense list (date, city, venue, ticket link only when supplied); an artist index with hover image reveal; a marquee of the newest release name.

**Type**: Unbounded or League Gothic display, Work Sans or Karla text, Space Mono for catalogue numbers.

**Motion**: expressive but optional: marquee, sleeves sliding; off under reduced motion.

**Imagery**: sleeves shot flat, live photos, the label's own art. **Avoid**: fake streaming counts, generic waveform graphics, invented press quotes.
