**Visitor**: needs something done, wants to know if you cover their area, roughly what it costs and how fast they can get you.

**Structure**: what you do and where, in the first line -> call or book button that is always reachable (sticky on phones) -> services with prices or "from" prices the creator gave -> examples of work (a salon's real cuts, a builder's real jobs) -> area covered and hours -> reviews only if real, with source -> FAQ -> contact.

**Mobile**: tap to call (`<a href="tel:...">`), tap for directions, booking in two taps. The phone is the main device.

**Visual**: match the trade's own culture. A barber can be bold and graphic; a dental clinic calm and clinical; a tattoo studio dark with the artists' flash as imagery. Use the business's real photos; stock trades photos are instantly recognisable.

**Don't**: invent certifications, years in business, "5 star" badges or review counts. If reviews live on another site, link to them rather than copying numbers.

**Storelib**: appointments are bookings with real availability; quotes are forms with service type, area and photos upload if supported.
