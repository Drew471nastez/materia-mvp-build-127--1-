**Visitor**: someone with a problem, often anxious, wanting to know whether this person can help and what it will cost.

**Style**: Swiss or editorial, quiet. Seed: Plain Counsel.

**Layout moves**: the questions people actually ask as headings with answers right below (an accordion for the long tail); the process as numbered steps; prices written plainly if the creator gave them; a portrait and a short honest bio; one contact or booking action repeated, never five.

**Type**: IBM Plex Serif or Literata display, IBM Plex Sans or Atkinson Hyperlegible text.

**Motion**: none; trust comes from clarity.

**Imagery**: the real people, their room, documents. **Avoid**: handshakes, skyline stock, scales of justice icons, "tailored solutions", invented case results.
