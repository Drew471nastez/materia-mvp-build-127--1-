**Visitor**: nearby, on a phone, wants hours, where, and what is out of the oven or on the menu today.

**Style**: organic or playful; a warm cream ground, a crust or espresso brown accent, maybe one fresh colour from the shop front. Seeds: Morning Crumb, Mise en Place.

**Layout moves**: a daily board (bake, time it comes out, sold out state as real text); hours and address pinned in the first screen; the menu as a printed card with dotted leaders; a slow marquee of today's names; beans or bread as products with origin, roast or flour in a small spec list.

**Type**: Fredoka or Young Serif display with Nunito Sans or Libre Franklin text; numerals tabular for prices and times.

**Motion**: expressive but small: a marquee, a press down button. Respect reduced motion.

**Imagery**: crumb and crust close ups, flour on counters, early light through the window, the actual staff. **Avoid**: stock latte art, feature cards ("Fresh daily"), invented reviews, hours only in the footer.
