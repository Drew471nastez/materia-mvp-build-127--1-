**Visitor**: someone who values how a thing is made, browsing slowly, often from social media.

**Style**: organic; clay, stone and linen tones, one fired glaze colour as the accent. Seeds: Clay Studio, Showroom Floor.

**Layout moves**: a process sequence (throwing, drying, glazing, firing) as a horizontal scroll gallery; products in small batches with a sold out state as text; a studio visit or workshop section that links to real bookings; an arch or kiln shaped image mask repeated as the one motif.

**Type**: Fraunces soft display with Karla text, or Gloock with Spectral for a more literary maker.

**Motion**: subtle; a slow reveal of each process step.

**Imagery**: hands at work, raw material, side light, the studio mess. **Avoid**: white background catalogue only, "handcrafted with love", invented waitlists.
