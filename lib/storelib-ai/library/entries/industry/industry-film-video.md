**Visitor**: a producer or brand looking at the work and who did it.

**Style**: dark cinematic with a precise grotesk. Seed: Second Reel.

**Layout moves**: a muted reel as the hero with a real pause button; a project index where each row shows a still on hover; stills in 2.39:1 letterbox; credits rolling as a real list of roles and names; a screening or festival list only with supplied dates.

**Type**: Schibsted Grotesk or League Gothic display, Work Sans or Karla text; credits in small tracked caps.

**Motion**: cinematic but controlled: slow fades, clip path reveals; all off under reduced motion; video never autoplays with sound.

**Imagery**: stills with grain, behind the scenes on set. **Avoid**: glowing gradients, festival laurels the work did not win, generic camera icons.
