**The trap**: centred badge, gradient headline, two buttons, a floating dashboard screenshot with glow, logo strip, three feature cards with icons, testimonial carousel, three tier pricing. Every model writes this. Do not.

**Instead**
- Lead with the job: "Invoices that chase themselves" plus a screenshot or short loop of the real product doing that job, at a readable size.
- Explain with the product: annotated screenshots, a step through of one workflow, a before and after of the task. Real UI beats illustrations.
- Features as a list grouped by job, with small screenshots, not 9 icon tiles.
- Pricing from what is actually sold. If there is one plan, show one plan with what it includes.
- Proof: real customer names with permission, real numbers from the creator.

**Visual directions**: a documentation feel (mono details, precise grid, light theme); a tool for creatives (bold type, colour from the product UI); a calm utility (plenty of white, a single strong accent from the logo). Dark with neon is allowed when the product is genuinely a dark UI and the concept says so.

**Storelib**: subscriptions are products with recurring prices; trials only if the product has one. A waitlist is a form, not a fake signup count.
