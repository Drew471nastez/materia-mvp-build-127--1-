**Visitor**: an enthusiast who reads specs, or a beginner who needs the right first kit or tour.

**Style**: industrial with editorial pacing. Seed: Field Guide.

**Layout moves**: spec tables for gear; routes or trips numbered with distance and difficulty from real data; a conditions strip only if the creator supplies data; a journal of trips as an editorial archive; a horizontal gallery of the place.

**Type**: Barlow Condensed display, IBM Plex Sans text, IBM Plex Mono for numbers.

**Motion**: subtle: parallax only on one landscape, off under reduced motion.

**Imagery**: wide landscapes with a small figure, gear in use, weather. **Avoid**: extreme sports stock, invented weather widgets, adrenaline copy.
