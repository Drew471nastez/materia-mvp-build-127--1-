**Visitor**: a buyer or a fan who wants to know what is in the bottle and where it comes from.

**Style**: luxury editorial, or a graphic label led look for natural wine and craft beer. Seed: Cellar Notes.

**Layout moves**: each bottle as a tall panel with vintage, grape, soil, alcohol and size in a spec list; a map or a year by year vintage list; tasting events only with real dates; age confirmation only where the store needs it.

**Type**: Gloock or Young Serif display with Spectral text; or Unbounded caps for a craft brewery.

**Motion**: subtle: label close ups that settle; nothing that sloshes.

**Imagery**: vineyards at dusk, cellar stone, hands and labels close. **Avoid**: gold gradients, invented medals and scores, "award winning" without the award.
