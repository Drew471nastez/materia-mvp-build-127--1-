**Visitor**: a client or a jury comparing studios, on a laptop more often than not, judging taste in the first ten seconds.

**Style**: Swiss or minimal; one neutral ground (bone, concrete grey, off white), one accent taken from a material (patinated copper, brick, oxidised green). Direction seed: Plan and Section.

**Layout moves**: a project index as a text list where the hovered row shows its image; each project a sequence of full bleed and two up images with a title block (year, location, area, status, team) set like a drawing sheet; a horizontal strip for process sketches; generous white space and very few words.

**Type**: Inter Tight or Archivo display set small and tight, or a quiet serif (Instrument Serif) for statements; data in IBM Plex Mono or DM Mono.

**Motion**: none to subtle; images crossfade on the index hover, no parallax on photos of buildings.

**Imagery**: overcast, straight verticals, people small in frame; drawings and models as real content. **Avoid**: renders presented as photos, three service cards ("Design, Build, Consult"), awards the studio did not win.
