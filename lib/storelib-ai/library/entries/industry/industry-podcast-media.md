**Visitor**: wants the latest episode, then the archive, then where to subscribe.

**Style**: creator with typography led energy. Seed: Booth Light.

**Layout moves**: the newest episode as the hero with its own player or a link to it; an episode index (number, guest, length, date) with search of the site's own; guests as a strip of portraits; subscribe links only to the platforms the creator gave.

**Type**: Red Hat Display throughout, or Bricolage Grotesque with Hanken Grotesk.

**Motion**: subtle: a level meter only while something plays.

**Imagery**: mics, guests mid laugh, the room. **Avoid**: fake download numbers, chart rankings that were not supplied, generic microphone icons in every heading.
