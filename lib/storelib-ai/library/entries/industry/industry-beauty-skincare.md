**Visitor**: wants to know what is in it, whether it suits their skin, and the price, then wants checkout to be quick.

**Style**: minimal contemporary ecommerce or dark luxury; stone, water and skin tones; one cool or one earthy accent. Seeds: Skin Lab, Velvet Room.

**Layout moves**: product first with a texture swatch; an ingredient table (name, what it does, percentage only when supplied); a routine as numbered steps with morning and evening tabs; before and after only with real, supplied photos and a slider that works by keyboard; the site's own cart drawer that shows the routine total.

**Type**: Sora or Instrument Sans for a clinical feel, Bodoni Moda or Cormorant for a fragrance house; text at 16 to 17px.

**Motion**: subtle: images that settle, a soft hover zoom on texture.

**Imagery**: product on stone and water, real skin without retouching, hands applying. **Avoid**: pastel gradients and blobs, "clean beauty" claims the store did not make, invented reviews, percentages of results.
