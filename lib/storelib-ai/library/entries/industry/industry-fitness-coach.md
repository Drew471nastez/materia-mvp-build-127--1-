**Visitor**: someone who has tried on their own and wants a person to follow. They judge on whether the coach looks credible for *their* body and goal, and how easy it is to start.

**Structure**: hero with who it is for and the starting action (book a call, start the programme) -> the coach (a real photo, a real story, qualifications that exist) -> how it works (consult, plan, check ins) -> programmes or packages from the store -> results only if real (client quotes with consent, before and after only if supplied) -> schedule or booking -> FAQ (equipment, level, injuries, cancellation) -> action again.

**Directions that are not the default** (the default is black, neon green, condensed caps, sweaty stock):
- *Quiet strength*: cream, olive, serif display, slow natural light photos. For mobility, pre and postnatal, older clients.
- *Training log*: off white, black rules, monospace numbers, a ticker of session times. For strength and powerlifting.
- *Sunrise club*: warm coral and sand, rounded grotesk, outdoor group shots. For run clubs and bootcamps.
- *Editorial athlete*: big cropped photography, narrow serif captions, magazine grid. For coaches with a strong personal image.

**Proof rules**: no invented transformations, no "500+ clients" unless the creator said so. Prefer a short real quote with a first name and goal over a carousel of five vague ones.

**Storelib**: programmes are digital products or courses; sessions are bookings; a members area is a community. Show the real price from the product.
