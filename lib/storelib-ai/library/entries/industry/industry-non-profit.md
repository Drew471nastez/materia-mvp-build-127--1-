**Visitor**: a possible donor, volunteer or someone who needs help. Many on phones, some with low vision or slow connections.

**Style**: editorial with warmth; a cream ground, a deep green or blue accent, high contrast everywhere. Seed: Common Ground.

**Layout moves**: big plain statements; one action per section (give, volunteer, get help); a table of where money goes using only numbers the organisation supplied; stories as documentary sequences with names only when given; an accessible donation flow (amount chips, then the real product or checkout link).

**Type**: Hanken Grotesk display with Atkinson Hyperlegible text; body 17 to 18px.

**Motion**: subtle, never needed to understand anything.

**Imagery**: real people, places and work, documentary. **Avoid**: stock suffering, invented impact numbers, progress bars toward goals that do not exist, counters that animate made up figures.
