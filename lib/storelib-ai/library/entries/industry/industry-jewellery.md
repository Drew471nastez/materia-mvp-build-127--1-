**Visitor**: comparing a considered purchase: material, size, price, delivery and returns.

**Style**: fashion and luxury, quiet. Seed: Gold Standard.

**Layout moves**: one piece per screen; macro detail followed by the piece worn; a ruler line showing real size; materials and care in a small table; size guide as an accordion; the site's own cart drawer with the engraving note if the product supports it.

**Type**: Bodoni Moda caps or Italiana display with Jost text; tiny tracked caps for labels and prices.

**Motion**: subtle: slow image settle, no glitter, no shimmer loops.

**Imagery**: stone, skin and hard shadow, macro focus. **Avoid**: sparkle animations, gradient gold text, invented celebrity mentions.
