**Visitor**: wants to see the work, pick an artist and book, and know about deposits and aftercare.

**Style**: dark with tall condensed type, or bright and graphic for a modern barber. Seed: Ink and Needle.

**Layout moves**: a dense flash grid you can open one at a time; an artist index with hover image reveal; healed work shown honestly; booking as a plain form with deposit and cancellation written out; aftercare as an accordion.

**Type**: League Gothic or Anton display with Karla text, Space Mono for prices and times.

**Motion**: subtle; type can be loud, motion should not be.

**Imagery**: real work on skin, the tools, the room. **Avoid**: skull clip art, generic razor icons, invented review counts.
