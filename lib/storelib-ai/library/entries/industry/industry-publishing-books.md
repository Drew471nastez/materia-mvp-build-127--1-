**Visitor**: a reader looking for the next thing to read or subscribe to; patient if the text is good.

**Style**: editorial and typography led; paper white, ink black, one red or blue. Seeds: Reading Room, Paper and Ink.

**Layout moves**: an issue style index on home (numbered, with authors and lengths); a catalogue list of titles with format, pages and price from the store; long reads in a 60 to 70 character column with marginal notes; a subscribe form that says exactly what arrives and how often; an archive by year.

**Type**: Literata or Source Serif 4 for both display and text, or Newsreader with a mono for metadata; drop caps and small caps used properly.

**Motion**: none; reading is the experience.

**Imagery**: covers shot flat, spreads, the author's own photo. **Avoid**: carousels of covers, "Join 10,000 readers" without the number, card grids of articles with identical excerpts.
