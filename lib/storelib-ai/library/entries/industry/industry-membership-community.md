**Visitor**: wants to know what happens after paying. "Join the community" with no detail is the most common failure.

**Structure**: who it is for and what members do together -> what you get, concretely and with frequency (weekly calls, monthly pack, private feed, discounts) -> a look inside: real screenshots or a description of a typical week, never fake member avatars -> the host -> price and billing period from the product -> how to leave (cancel any time, if true) -> FAQ -> join.

**Tiers**: only if the store has them. Show tiers side by side with differences highlighted, not repeated features.

**Visual**: a community is people; use the creator's real photos of events or members who agreed. Warmer palettes, readable type, less spectacle. A club for a niche (film photographers, runners) should borrow the niche's own visual culture.

**Don't**: show member counts unless real ("Join 10,000+ members"), stacks of random avatar circles, generic "network with like minded people" copy.

**Storelib**: a membership is a recurring product; the community is a Storelib community the membership unlocks. Link the join action to the real checkout or join flow and show the real price.
