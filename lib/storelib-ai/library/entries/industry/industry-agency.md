**Visitor**: a potential client checking taste, relevant experience and whether the people seem good to work with.

**Structure**: a statement of what the studio believes or does, set large -> selected work (three to six case studies, real clients only) -> services as a plain list with one line each, not icon tiles -> approach in a few numbered steps only if the steps are genuinely sequential -> people (real photos) -> recognition only if real -> contact with a direct email and what to include in a brief.

**Visual**: agencies are where trend chasing is most visible. Choose a direction that matches the studio's actual output: a branding studio can be type led and bold; an engineering studio can be precise and Swiss; a production company can be cinematic dark with video. Show their work large; the site frame should be quieter than the work.

**Signature moves** that fit: a running list of clients as a typographic marquee (real clients only), a large index of projects with hover previews, case study pages with project specific colour.

**Avoid**: "We craft digital experiences", stats like "200+ projects" without basis, three service cards with icons, stock team handshake photos, a contact form with 12 fields.
