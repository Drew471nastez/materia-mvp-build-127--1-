**Visitor**: a client or hiring manager scanning for fit in under a minute, then reading one case study deeply.

**Home**: name and what you do in one line -> the work, immediately. Options: a project index list (title, client, year, discipline) with a preview image on hover; a large image grid; a single featured project per screen. Then a short about and contact.

**Case study rhythm**: title and one line outcome -> role, team, year in a small meta row -> the problem in two or three sentences -> large images in alternating widths (full, two up, inset with caption) -> what changed, with real results only -> next project link.

**Make it memorable through one move**, not ten: a custom cursor label on project hover, a typographic index, an oversized name in the footer, a colour per project. Pick one.

**Do**: fast loading images (srcset, lazy below the fold), captions that explain decisions, contact as an email link that works.
**Don't**: skill bars and percentage charts, logo walls of clients you did not work with, a "services" grid of icons, long intros before the work, scroll jacking.
