**Good SaaS style is product photography for software**: the UI is the hero, shown large, crisp and annotated.

**Specs**: one sans (Inter, Geist-like, or a grotesk with character) with tabular numerals; a 4px or 8px spacing scale; neutral surfaces; one brand accent from the product's own UI; radius matching the product UI (often 6 to 10px).

**Do**: real screenshots at 1x or 2x with `srcset`; short looping clips of a workflow (muted, no autoplay on slow connections, poster image); feature sections that alternate text and screenshot with real captions; a changelog or docs link as proof of activity.

**Don't**: purple to blue gradients, glowing orbs behind the hero, a fake dashboard of random charts, nine feature icons, "Trusted by" logos without permission.

**Variations**: a docs-like engineering style (mono labels, thin rules, light background), a creative tool style (bold colour, big type, playful), an enterprise style (calm navy and white, dense information, comparison tables).
