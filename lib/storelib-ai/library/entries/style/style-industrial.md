**Signals**: monospace or DIN-like type, part numbers and spec tables, thin rules and grid lines, concrete or metal photography, safety orange `#ff5a1f` or hi-vis yellow `#e6ff00` as the single accent on grey or off white.

**Use for**: workwear, tools, hardware, bikes, coffee roasters, producers selling gear, technical courses.

**Components**: spec tables with tabular numbers; labels like `REF 004 / 240G`; buttons as rectangles with mono caps; status chips only when they carry real state (in stock from data).

**Layout**: dense information is fine when organised. Use a strict grid, consistent column widths for numbers, and plenty of contrast.

**Don't**: invent technical specs. Every number shown comes from the product's fields.
