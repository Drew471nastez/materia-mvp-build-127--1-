**Signals**: soft but readable contrast (text still 4.5:1), warm or mineral neutrals, rounded but not pill shaped components (8 to 16px), humanist sans or a gentle serif, natural light photography, slow fades.

**Palettes that are not sage and beige**: stone and slate (`#e9e6e1`, `#3e4644`); clay and ink (`#efe3d6`, `#2b2522`, accent `#b5643c`); night bath (`#1e2a2a`, `#d9e2dc`, accent `#c9a46a`).

**Type**: a humanist sans for UI and body at 17 to 18px; display in a soft serif. Avoid script fonts for anything longer than a word.

**Motion**: fades and small upward drifts (8 to 12px) at 400 to 600ms; no bounces, no parallax shaking.

**Do**: make practicalities easy to find (time, place, price, what to bring). Calm comes from clarity.

**Don't**: lotus icons, gradient blobs, stock meditation photos, vague healing claims.
