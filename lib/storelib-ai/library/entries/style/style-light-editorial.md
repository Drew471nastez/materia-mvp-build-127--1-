**Signals**: warm white background (`#fbfaf7` or `#f7f4ee`), near black ink (`#1b1a17`), a book serif for body at 18 to 20px, a display serif or grotesk for headings, hairline rules, captions in a small sans.

**Measure**: 60 to 70 characters, line height 1.55 to 1.7 for body. Paragraph spacing equal to line height; no indents unless set like a book.

**Colour**: one muted accent (ink blue `#2a4a7f`, brick `#9a3b26`) for links and small markers. Links underlined with offset 3px and thickness 1px.

**Use for**: writers, newsletters, publishers, thoughtful brands, course creators with long sales pages.

**Avoid**: grey body text below 4.5:1, centred long text, cards around articles.
