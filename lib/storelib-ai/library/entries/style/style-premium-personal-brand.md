**Signals**: a professionally lit portrait at large size, an editorial serif or refined grotesk, a restrained palette taken from the person's wardrobe or book covers, selected appearances and writing as proof.

**Structure**: name and what they are known for -> the main offer (book, speaking, advisory, course) -> selected work or appearances (real) -> about -> writing or newsletter -> contact for bookings with what to include.

**Moves that feel premium**: a portrait that bleeds off one edge with the name set across it; a list of talks as an elegant index with years; quotes from real press in italic serif.

**Don't**: "World renowned", "visionary" or invented logos of media outlets. Do not add a fake stats strip (countries, audiences reached) unless the person supplied the numbers.
