**Visitor**: a fan or a new follower arriving from social. Usually on a phone, short attention, wants the latest thing and a way to support.

**Structure**: the creator (photo, name, one line) -> the newest or main offer (product, course, membership, community) -> other offers in priority order -> latest content (videos, posts from the blog) -> newsletter -> socials.

**Visual**: borrow the creator's channel identity: their colours, thumbnail style, typeface. A creator site should feel like their videos, not like a template.

**Do**: big tap targets, offers with real prices, a short about in their own voice.

**Don't**: invent follower counts, brand logos ("as seen on") that are not real, or a wall of identical link buttons when the store has richer content to show.
