**Signals**: whole sections in one saturated colour; a palette of three or four flat colours used as fields, not accents everywhere; hard offset shadows; cut out product shots; thick borders.

**Layouts**: colour changes at each section to mark chapters; a sticker like badge only where it carries real news; large type knocked out of colour fields.

**Type**: Soft rounded, Wide display or Characterful grotesk systems.

**Do**: check text contrast on each colour field (4.5:1); keep one neutral for long text.

**Don't**: purple to blue gradients, glassmorphism, blurred blobs, emoji as icons; those are a different, overused look.

**Combines with**: playful, neo-brutalism, kids and education, food, community.
