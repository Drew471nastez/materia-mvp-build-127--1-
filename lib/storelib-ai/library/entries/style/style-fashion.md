**Signals**: full bleed campaign photography, small uppercase navigation, product grids with no visible containers, a wide display face or a condensed sans, captions with product names that link to products.

**Lookbook pattern**: a sequence of large images, each with a small caption listing the pieces (linked to real products). Alternate full bleed and two up. On phones, swipe horizontally within a look.

**Type**: navigation and labels 11 to 13px caps, tracking 0.08 to 0.14em; product names 13 to 15px; display used sparingly and big.

**Colour**: from the season's campaign; neutrals for the frame. A collection can recolour the header and accent.

**Motion**: image hover swaps to the second product image (crossfade 200 to 300ms); slow scale on campaign images; marquee for a drop announcement.

**Avoid**: rounded product cards, shadows, star ratings, sticker badges, stock models.
