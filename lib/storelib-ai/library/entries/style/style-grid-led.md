**Signals**: a 12 column grid you can feel; 1px rules between rows and columns; numbers and labels in small caps or mono; images cropped to grid units; text and images aligned on the same lines.

**Layouts**: an index table of projects or products; a two column spec sheet; a modular grid where one cell spans two for emphasis (not a bento of equal cards); a sticky left column with section numbers.

**Type**: Swiss grotesk or Newsprint systems (typography-type-library); tabular numerals; tight display tracking.

**Do**: let density vary: a sparse statement row after a dense table.

**Don't**: put every item in an equal rounded card; the grid is lines and alignment, not boxes.

**Combines with**: swiss, editorial, industrial, architecture and shop catalogues.
