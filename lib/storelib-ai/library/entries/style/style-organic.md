**Signals**: earth palettes (clay `#c46b4a`, moss `#5b6b3a`, sand `#e8dcc8`, bark `#3b2f26`), paper textures used lightly, irregular image masks (arches, soft blobs used once), a warm serif or a humanist sans, photography of materials and hands.

**Use for**: ceramics, food producers, florists, sustainable brands, herbalists, craft courses.

**Shapes**: an arch top image (`border-radius: 999px 999px 0 0`) or one hand drawn divider is enough. Do not mask every image differently.

**Motion**: slow, gentle, mostly fades; a slight rotation on hover for objects can feel handmade.

**Don't**: leaf icons beside every heading, "eco" green gradients, unverified sustainability claims (carbon neutral, 100% organic) unless the creator states them.
