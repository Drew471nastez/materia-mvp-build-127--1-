**Signals**: real people training, high contrast, condensed or heavy display type, numbers used visually (sets, reps, times), rhythm from marquees or schedules.

**Beyond the default**: the default is black, neon green and all caps condensed. Alternatives: a lab style (white, mono numbers, measurement rules) for performance coaches; a sunrise club style (warm coral, sand, rounded grotesk) for community training; a heritage boxing gym style (cream, red, slab serif, poster layouts).

**Type**: display heavy or condensed at 56 to 140px; body 16 to 18px; tabular numerals for schedules and stats.

**Imagery**: crop tight on effort (hands, chalk, breath), use motion blur sparingly, no stock models posing with dumbbells.

**Motion**: quick (150 to 250ms) and snappy; counters only for real numbers; marquee for class times.

**Don't**: before and after photos without consent and source, fake stats, shouty copy in every heading.
