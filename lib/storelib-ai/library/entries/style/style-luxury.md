**Signals**: large uninterrupted imagery, generous margins (section padding 120 to 200px desktop), small precise type, capitals with wide tracking for labels, slow eased motion.

**Type**: display in a high contrast serif or a light wide grotesk at 40 to 96px; labels 11 to 13px caps at 0.12 to 0.2em tracking; body 16px with 1.6 to 1.7 line height.

**Palette**: ivory `#f5f1ea`, charcoal `#1d1c1a`, one deep colour (oxblood `#5a1a1a`, bottle `#1f3a2e`). Accent only on hover and focus lines, never as large fills.

**Components**: text buttons with an underline or thin outlined buttons (1px, radius 0); product cards without borders; image reveals with clip-path masks.

**Avoid**: badges, sale tags, stars, pills, more than one call to action per screen, gold gradients, glow.

**Combines with**: editorial (magazine spreads with luxury pacing), minimalism, dark cinematic (for fragrance or night collections).
