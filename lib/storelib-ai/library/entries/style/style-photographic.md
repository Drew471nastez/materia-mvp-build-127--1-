**Signals**: full bleed and full screen images, images shown at their own ratio, sequences that pace like a book, small precise captions, almost no interface chrome.

**Layouts**: one image per screen; diptychs; a horizontal sequence (example ai_hscroll_gallery); a text index with hover image reveal (ai_hover_reveal_list); a slow parallax band (ai_parallax_media) once per page at most.

**Type**: small and exact (Gallery label system) or a single large italic serif statement.

**Do**: use the creator's own photographs; write alt text that says what is in the picture; size images with width and height to avoid layout shift.

**Don't**: dark overlays on every image, stock photography, text over busy parts of a photo without a scrim.

**Combines with**: minimalism, portfolio, luxury, travel and hotels, food.
