**Signals**: everything locks to a column grid; type is flush left, ragged right; hierarchy through size and weight, not colour; generous negative space on one side; numbers and labels used structurally.

**Specs**: 12 column grid, 24px gutters, margins at least one column; a neo grotesk (Inter, Helvetica-like, Neue Haas-like families available in the theme) in 2 to 3 weights; scale ratio about 1.333; headlines tight (-0.02 to -0.03em), body 0.

**Colour**: black, white, one primary (signal red `#e2231a`, blue `#0046ad`) used in blocks or for one element per screen.

**Moves**: oversized numerals as section markers when the order matters, rotated vertical labels, rules spanning the grid, images cropped to grid units.

**Don't**: centre layouts, add drop shadows, rounded corners, decorative icons.

**Combines with**: editorial, brutalism (Swiss grid with raw elements), industrial (technical specs and mono).
