**Signals**: a display serif or a characterful grotesk at large sizes, a clear text column (60 to 72 characters), captions, bylines, issue or section labels that carry information, images with varied widths.

**Layout**: a 12 column grid where text sits in 6 to 7 columns offset from the left; images alternate between full bleed, inset with caption and two up. Use rules (1px lines) to separate, not cards.

**Type moves**: drop caps on long reads, pull quotes set at 1.5 to 2x body in italic, small caps for labels, tabular numerals in data.

**Do**: write real captions; they are part of the design. Vary rhythm: a dense text spread followed by a full image.

**Don't**: fake magazine furniture ("Issue 01", "Vol. 3") unless it means something to the brand. Do not centre every text block.

**Combines with**: luxury (slower, more space), Swiss (grid discipline, sans display), fashion (image led lookbook spreads), dark cinematic (night editions).
