**Signals**: product grids with consistent image ratios on light neutral backgrounds (`#f3f2ef` behind cut out products works well), small type for name and price, brand personality in the header, hero and one accent, quick add buttons that appear on hover (desktop) and stay visible (touch).

**Product grid**: 2 columns at phone, 3 at tablet, 4 at desktop; 12 to 24px gaps; name 14 to 15px, price 14px tabular; colour swatches only if the product has colour variants.

**Product page**: gallery left (60%), details right sticky on desktop; variant pickers as buttons, not selects, when there are few options; add to cart full width on phones; shipping and returns as an accordion from real policies.

**Cart**: a drawer with line items, quantity steppers, subtotal and checkout; empty state with a link back to the shop.

**Avoid**: fake scarcity, fake reviews, popups in the first five seconds, carousels as the main way to browse.
