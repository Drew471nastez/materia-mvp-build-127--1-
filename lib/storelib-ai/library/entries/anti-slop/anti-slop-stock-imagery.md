**Clichés**: handshake, team high five, laptop and coffee flat lay, person pointing at a whiteboard, smiling headset support agent, lightbulb, rocket, puzzle pieces, glowing brain, generic city skyline, model lifting a dumbbell in a clean gym, lotus pose on a beach.

**Evaluator checks**: image alt text and file names matching cliché terms; the same placeholder service used for every image; hero images with no relation to the stated industry.

**Fix**: use the creator's own photos first. When searching open licence images (the AI builder uses Openverse), write queries from the concept's imagery direction: subject, light, crop, mood ("chalk dusted hands on a barbell, side light, close crop"). Fewer, better images beat many generic ones. Credit images as their licence requires.
