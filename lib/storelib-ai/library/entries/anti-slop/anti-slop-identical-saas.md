**The sequence**: hero, logo cloud, "Features" with three or six icon cards, two alternating image and text rows, testimonial carousel, three pricing tiers with the middle one raised, FAQ, gradient CTA band, four column footer.

**Evaluator checks**: three or more repeated sibling blocks with identical structure (icon plus heading plus paragraph), multiple sections of the same shape in a row, the presence of most items in the sequence.

**Fix**: start from what the visitor needs (core-content-hierarchy). Show the actual product or work instead of describing features; use a list instead of cards; let the number of items come from the content (two, four, seven); merge sections that say the same thing; drop the logo cloud if there are no real logos; vary section density and alignment.
