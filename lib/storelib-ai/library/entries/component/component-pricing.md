**Number of plans comes from the store.** One offer: a single focused panel with what is included. Two: side by side. Three: the classic row, with the recommended one marked only if the creator recommends it.

**Each plan**: name, price with period (from the product), who it is for in one line, 3 to 7 included items (specific: "4 live calls a month", not "Premium support"), action.

**Highlight**: a border or background shift and a text label "Recommended" (not a glowing gradient card raised 20px).

**Billing toggle**: monthly and yearly only if both prices exist. Show the real difference, not an invented "Save 20%".

**Comparison table**: for 3 or more plans with many features: rows of features, columns of plans, check marks with text alternatives, sticky header row on scroll.

**Phones**: stack plans; put the recommended one first only if that matches the DOM order; keep the price visible near each button.

**Avoid**: fake strikethrough prices, "Most popular" without data, more than 7 bullet points per plan.
