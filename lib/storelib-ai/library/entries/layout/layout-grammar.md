Choose a composition per section from what the section holds (content volume, real photos, the job on the page), not from habit. Vary width, density, photo intensity and type scale between neighbours on one grid and one set of tokens. A composition that needs photos the creator does not have is the wrong one: switch to a type-led composition instead of showing an empty frame or a random landscape.

- **full-bleed-photo-hero** (Edge-to-edge photographic opening): one real photograph fills the first screen, the promise set where the photo is quiet, the action in reach. Works when the creator has a strong real photo of the person, place or work. Not when no real photo yet (use type-led-hero); a stock-looking crop. Real photos: 1. Phone: a separate tall crop with the subject in the top two thirds, text below the subject or on a solid band, the button in the first screen. Examples: ai_video_hero, ai_image_reveal.
- **split-editorial** (Asymmetric editorial split): a 7/5 or 8/4 split: a cropped photo on one side, a typographic column on the other, bleeding to the viewport edge on the photo side. Works when a person or product plus a clear paragraph of why. Not when four splits in a row; 50/50 by habit; a tall image beside two short lines. Real photos: 1. Phone: written in phone order: photo first for products and portfolios, words first for services; the photo gets its own ratio (4/5). Examples: ai_image_reveal.
- **type-led-hero** (Type-led opening): the statement is the image: very large type set on purpose, a supporting line and one action, no photo needed. Works when no strong photo, a sharp promise, or a confident voice. Not when a centred headline over three cards; a generic promise. Real photos: none. Phone: line breaks chosen per line (blocks), a smaller clamp() step, the action in the first screen. Examples: ai_mask_reveal, ai_split_text.
- **manifesto** (Manifesto with quiet evidence): an oversized multi-line statement of what the business believes, then two or three plain facts under it. Works when a point of view worth stating: a method, a refusal, a standard. Not when fog words; a statement any business could sign. Real photos: none. Phone: the statement keeps its size relative to the screen, the facts become a short list. Examples: ai_mask_reveal.
- **narrow-reading-column** (Narrow reading column): a 60 to 68 character column with real rhythm: a story, a philosophy, a letter, with a pull quote or margin note. Works when the creator wrote something worth reading. Not when padding a page with filler paragraphs. Real photos: none. Phone: the same column at full width with a larger body size and generous leading. Examples: ai_scroll_reveal.
- **visual-dossier** (Visual dossier): alternating full-width and inset photos with captions, like a printed feature. Works when five or more real photos of the work, space or process. Not when fewer than four photos; stock imagery. Real photos: 4. Phone: one photo per screen, captions under each, insets go full width. Examples: ai_image_reveal, ai_parallax_media.
- **sticky-narrative** (Narrative sticky visual): a picture column stays while two to five steps pass beside it. Works when a method, process or programme with distinct steps and a picture for each. Not when steps without pictures; more than one per page on a quiet site. Real photos: 2. Phone: plain sequential steps, each with its own picture. Examples: ai_sticky_story.
- **unequal-mosaic** (Restrained mosaic): a gallery of unequal proportions on an underlying grid, the hero image larger. Works when a body of visual work in different ratios. Not when a uniform grid of identical squares; fewer than five images. Real photos: 5. Phone: two columns of unequal height, or a sideways strip. Examples: ai_hscroll_gallery.
- **index-list** (Crisp index): a typographic list of projects, services, programmes or packs, each row a link, a picture on hover. Works when several items a visitor chooses between. Not when items without their own page or detail. Real photos: none. Phone: rows with a small picture each; no hover-only content. Examples: ai_hover_reveal_list, ai_hover_lift.
- **unequal-offers** (Unequal offer strip): two or three real offers with deliberately different weights, the recommended one larger, real prices from the store. Works when the creator sells packages, programmes or plans. Not when three identical price cards; invented prices. Real photos: none. Phone: the recommended offer first, the others as compact rows. Examples: ai_hover_lift, ai_sticky_stack.
- **photo-quote** (Photographic quote): one real quote with a real name and a photo, set large. Works when the store has a real review or the creator supplied one with permission. Not when any invented testimonial; a carousel of five vague ones. Real photos: 1. Phone: photo above, the quote at reading size.
- **case-study** (Full-bleed case study): one project told big: a wide photo, the brief, what was done, real outcomes, annotations. Works when real project detail exists. Not when made-up results or metrics. Real photos: 2. Phone: photo, then the brief and outcome as a short list. Examples: ai_image_reveal.
- **process-steps** (Numbered process): three to five numbered steps of how working together goes. Works when the visitor's question is what happens after I click. Not when steps that say nothing (Step 1: Contact us). Real photos: none. Phone: a vertical list with the numbers large. Examples: ai_scroll_reveal, ai_sticky_story.
- **anchored-detail** (Anchored detail with sticky contents): a sticky table of contents beside long service or programme detail. Works when a lot of real detail (inclusions, schedules, policies). Not when short pages. Real photos: none. Phone: the contents become a short jump list at the top.
- **booking-cta** (Minimal booking action): one sentence, one real action (the booking form, the calendar link, the store's product), and what happens next. Works when the site's job is a booking or a consultation. Not when a button to nowhere; a promise of a booking system that does not exist. Real photos: none. Phone: the action full width at the bottom of the screen.
- **product-editorial** (Long-form product editorial): a product told like a feature: big image, what it is, how it is made, specs, the real buy button. Works when a hero product from the store. Not when products that are not in the store. Real photos: 2. Phone: image, title, price and buy button in the first screen, the story below. Examples: ai_cursor_tilt, ai_image_reveal.
- **product-led-demo** (Product-led demonstration): the real product or a real screenshot with accessible controls that show what it does (tabs, before and after, a player). Works when the thing sold can be shown working: software, a pack, a preset, a course. Not when fake app UI; a demo of features the product does not have. Real photos: 1. Phone: controls large enough to tap, one state per screen. Examples: ai_tabs, ai_before_after.
- **credentials** (Real credentials): qualifications, clients, publications or press that actually exist, set plainly. Works when the creator supplied them. Not when logo strips of companies that never worked with them. Real photos: none. Phone: a compact list, not a logo wall.
- **comparison-table** (Comparison table): a real table of what each offer includes. Works when two or more offers with genuinely different contents. Not when one offer; invented tiers. Real photos: none. Phone: one offer per row with a toggle between them, or a sideways scroll with the first column fixed. Examples: ai_tabs.
- **compact-faq** (Compact FAQ): the questions people really ask, strong type, one open at a time. Works when there are real practical questions (equipment, level, delivery, licences). Not when questions nobody asks; the default closer of every page. Real photos: none. Phone: full width rows, large tap targets. Examples: ai_faq_accordion.
- **marquee-band** (Running band of real words): a slow line of dates, releases or today's list between two sections. Works when real repeating facts (tour dates, class times, drops). Not when slogans; more than one per page. Real photos: none. Phone: same, with the pause switch reachable. Examples: ai_marquee.
- **media-reel** (Work reel): the creator's own video or audio first, with native controls. Works when film, music, performance: the work is moving image or sound. Not when autoplaying sound; a stock video. Real photos: none. Phone: a poster frame and a play control, no autoplay on cellular. Examples: ai_video_hero.
- **signature-footer** (Brand-signature footer): the name set large, real contact options, menus, sign up, language and currency. Works when always: every site ends on its own footer. Not when a default theme footer. Real photos: none. Phone: contact first, menus collapsed. Examples: ai_site_footer.
