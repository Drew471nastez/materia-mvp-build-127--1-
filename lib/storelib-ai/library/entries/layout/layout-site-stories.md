Plan the home page as this business's story, not a template. The site plan is checked: a home that is the generic Hero, Features, Pricing, FAQ, CTA skeleton of a software landing page is refused for any business that is not software, and the refusal names the story to tell. Each line is a beat (what the section does for the visitor) and the composition that suits it (layout-grammar). Adapt the order and drop beats the creator has nothing real for (no testimonial without a real review).

**fitness-coach** (Fitness coach or personal trainer). Action: book, a consultation: the store's booking form (a form with booking) or a first-session product's checkout_url. Motion feel: balanced. Must cover: the coach (a real person), who it is for, the method, booking a consultation.
1. who it is for and the first step, over real training photography (full-bleed-photo-hero)
2. the coach: a real photo, a short real story, qualifications that exist (split-editorial)
3. who the programme is for, and who it is not for (manifesto)
4. the method: assessment, plan, sessions, check ins (sticky-narrative)
5. a running band of real class times or the next intake date (marquee-band)
6. the offers from the store, the recommended one larger (unequal-offers)
7. a training day in photos (visual-dossier)
8. one real client quote with consent, only if supplied (photo-quote)
9. what to bring, level, injuries, cancellation (compact-faq)
10. book the consultation, and what happens next (booking-cta)
Authentic training photography is the main asset: performance and sport energy, never a literary magazine. Proof only with permission. Other pages: coaching or programmes (the offers in detail); about (the coach); contact or book.

**wellness-studio** (Pilates, yoga or wellness studio). Action: book, a class or first visit: the booking form, or the intro offer's checkout_url. Motion feel: quiet. Must cover: the classes, the teachers, the space or location, booking.
1. the room and the feeling of a class, one calm photo (full-bleed-photo-hero)
2. what a first visit is like, step by step (process-steps)
3. the classes and programmes, by level (index-list)
4. the teachers and their training (split-editorial)
5. the space: light, equipment, where it is (visual-dossier)
6. this week's timetable or the next course start (marquee-band)
7. the intro offer and passes from the store (unequal-offers)
8. accessibility, injuries, pregnancy, what to wear (compact-faq)
9. a short note from the founder (narrow-reading-column)
10. book a first class, and the address if given (booking-cta)
Softer rhythm, restrained motion, accessible trust. Atmosphere comes from real photos of the room, not gradients. Other pages: classes and timetable; teachers; pricing and passes; visit or contact.

**sample-pack-store** (Music producer or sample pack store). Action: buy, buy: add to cart (data-storelib-add-to-cart with the product id) or the pack's checkout_url. Motion feel: expressive. Must cover: the packs from the store, listening to the sound, what is in a pack (files, formats, licence).
1. the newest pack as the opening: cover, name, price, a play control (product-editorial)
2. listen: previews of the real audio (product-led-demo)
3. the catalogue as an index of packs with format, BPM and key (index-list)
4. a running band of releases or what is new (marquee-band)
5. how it was recorded: gear, rooms, the process (visual-dossier)
6. the producer: who makes these and why (split-editorial)
7. what you get: files, formats, sizes, licence (comparison-table)
8. one featured pack told in depth (case-study)
9. licence, refunds, downloads (compact-faq)
10. sign up for new releases (booking-cta)
The sound is the product: previews and real specs come before any story. Digital checkout is Storelib's own; no fake download counts. Other pages: shop (every pack); the product template; licence; about.

**agency** (Design, brand or creative agency). Action: enquire, an enquiry: the contact form, or a mailto link the creator gave. Motion feel: expressive. Must cover: the work first, a point of view, an enquiry.
1. a statement of what the studio does and for whom (type-led-hero)
2. selected work as an index with hover previews (index-list)
3. one project told big, with the brief and what changed (case-study)
4. the work, in a mosaic of real images (unequal-mosaic)
5. what the studio believes about the work (manifesto)
6. services with what each includes (anchored-detail)
7. how a project runs, week by week (process-steps)
8. real clients or press, if any (credentials)
9. the people (split-editorial)
10. start a project: the form and what happens next (booking-cta)
Projects first; images deserve large surfaces. No invented client logos or results. Other pages: work (index); a case study page per project; studio; contact.

**architect** (Architecture or interior studio). Action: enquire, an enquiry form or the studio's email. Motion feel: balanced. Must cover: projects first, the studio's philosophy, an enquiry.
1. one built project, edge to edge (full-bleed-photo-hero)
2. the projects as an index by type and year (index-list)
3. a project told through plans and photos (case-study)
4. details and materials (visual-dossier)
5. the studio's philosophy (narrow-reading-column)
6. services and stages of work (process-steps)
7. awards or publications that exist (credentials)
8. the team (split-editorial)
9. current and upcoming work (unequal-mosaic)
10. enquire about a project (booking-cta)
Large image surfaces, quiet type, real plans and drawings. Other pages: projects; a page per project; studio; contact.

**photographer** (Photographer or videographer). Action: book, a date enquiry or booking form. Motion feel: expressive. Must cover: the work or reel, booking a date.
1. the reel or the strongest frame (media-reel)
2. selected stories, an index with previews (index-list)
3. one story told in full (case-study)
4. a mosaic of frames at their own ratios (unequal-mosaic)
5. how they work on the day (sticky-narrative)
6. the collections or packages (unequal-offers)
7. about the photographer, a self portrait (split-editorial)
8. one real couple's or client's words (photo-quote)
9. dates, travel, delivery (compact-faq)
10. check a date (booking-cta)
The work is the hero, shown at its own ratio and never cropped to fit a card. Other pages: work; packages; about; contact.

**digital-creator** (Digital creator or educator). Action: buy, the product's checkout_url, the community's join link, or the subscribe form. Motion feel: balanced. Must cover: the creator's personality, real products or the community.
1. the creator and the one thing they help with (split-editorial)
2. the flagship product from the store, told in depth (product-editorial)
3. what is inside: modules, templates, pages (product-led-demo)
4. all products as an index (index-list)
5. the community or membership, if there is one (full-bleed-photo-hero)
6. free content to start with (narrow-reading-column)
7. a running band of what is new (marquee-band)
8. real reviews from the store, if any (photo-quote)
9. refunds, access, updates (compact-faq)
10. join the list or buy (booking-cta)
A strong route into actual products with Storelib checkout. Other pages: shop; the product template; about; community.

**saas** (Software product). Action: join, sign up or start a trial on a real page, or a demo request form. Motion feel: balanced. Must cover: the product shown working.
1. the problem and the product in one line, with a true screenshot (split-editorial)
2. the product working: real screenshots with controls (product-led-demo)
3. the workflow, step by step (sticky-narrative)
4. honest differences from the alternatives (comparison-table)
5. features as an index, not a grid of icons (index-list)
6. who uses it, only if real (credentials)
7. pricing from real plans (unequal-offers)
8. questions about data, billing, migration (compact-faq)
9. a note from the founder (narrow-reading-column)
10. start (booking-cta)
A true product screenshot, never a fake app UI. Other pages: pricing; product or features; about; contact.

**local-professional** (Local professional or trade). Action: enquire, call or the contact form; the address and hours only if given. Motion feel: quiet. Must cover: the services, contact details.
1. what they do, where, and how to reach them, in the first screen (split-editorial)
2. services with what each costs or includes (anchored-detail)
3. how an appointment or job goes (process-steps)
4. qualifications and memberships that exist (credentials)
5. the person or team (split-editorial)
6. areas served or the premises (visual-dossier)
7. real reviews from the store, if any (photo-quote)
8. prices, timing, cancellations (compact-faq)
9. a short note on how they work (narrow-reading-column)
10. contact: phone, form, hours (booking-cta)
Clarity, trust, contact and service details above fancy interactions. Other pages: services; about; contact.

**artist** (Artist or maker). Action: buy, the store's works (add to cart or checkout_url), or an enquiry for commissions. Motion feel: balanced. Must cover: the work, the artist's voice.
1. one work, large, with its title (full-bleed-photo-hero)
2. the works for sale from the store (index-list)
3. a series told in depth (case-study)
4. the studio and process (sticky-narrative)
5. the artist's own words (narrow-reading-column)
6. works in a mosaic at their own ratios (unequal-mosaic)
7. exhibitions and stockists that exist (credentials)
8. commissions: how they work (process-steps)
9. shipping, framing, care (compact-faq)
10. buy or enquire (booking-cta)
Work focus and personal voice; motion and type can express the practice. Other pages: works or shop; about; commissions; contact.

**food-place** (Restaurant, cafe or bakery). Action: book, a table booking form, the order link the creator gave, or the store's products. Motion feel: balanced. Must cover: the menu, hours and location.
1. the room or the food, one real photo (full-bleed-photo-hero)
2. today's menu or bakes as a running band (marquee-band)
3. the menu, set like a menu (index-list)
4. how it is made, in photos (visual-dossier)
5. the people behind it (split-editorial)
6. opening hours and where (anchored-detail)
7. private dining or events (unequal-offers)
8. a short story of the place (narrow-reading-column)
9. allergens, groups, bookings (compact-faq)
10. book a table or order (booking-cta)
Real food and room photography; hours and address only if supplied. Other pages: menu; visit; about.

**product-brand** (Product brand or shop). Action: buy, add to cart with real product ids, collections from the store. Motion feel: balanced. Must cover: real products from the store.
1. the product or collection in use, edge to edge (full-bleed-photo-hero)
2. the bestsellers from the store (index-list)
3. one product told in depth with the real buy button (product-editorial)
4. the collections (unequal-mosaic)
5. how it is made (sticky-narrative)
6. a running band of what is new or delivery news (marquee-band)
7. the brand's point of view (manifesto)
8. real reviews from the store, if any (photo-quote)
9. delivery, returns, care (compact-faq)
10. sign up for drops (booking-cta)
Products from the store only; no invented items or prices. Other pages: shop; collections; the product template; about.
