- **Alt text**: what the image shows that matters here. A product photo: "Olive linen overshirt, front view". A coach portrait on the about page: "Sam Reid in the studio". Decorative textures: `alt=""`. Do not start with "Image of".
- **Link text**: unique and meaningful out of context. Several "Read more" links get a visually hidden suffix ("Read more about the spring retreat").
- **Plain language**: short sentences, common words, explain jargon once. Helps everyone, especially non native speakers.
- **Not colour alone**: sale prices, errors, availability and required fields also use text or icons.
- **Not position alone**: "click the button on the right" fails on phones and screen readers.
- **Numbers and dates**: write them so they read naturally ("14 June 2026"), use `<time datetime>`.
