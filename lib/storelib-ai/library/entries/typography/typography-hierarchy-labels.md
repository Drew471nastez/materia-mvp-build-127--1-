Small text is useful when it carries information the heading does not: a date, a category, a step number that matters, a location, a price, a reading time.

**Earns its place**: "Saturday 14 June, Leeds" above an event name. "Module 3 of 8" above a lesson title. "Photo: Ana Ruiz" under an image.

**Does not**: "Our services" above a heading that says "Services". "Features" above "Everything you need". Numbered markers 01, 02, 03 on sections that are not sequential.

**Styling**: 12 to 14px, either uppercase with +0.08 to +0.14em tracking or sentence case in a muted colour that still passes 4.5:1. Plain text, not a pill or chip with a background. Keep 8 to 12px between label and heading.

**Captions**: 13 to 14px, muted, max 60ch, directly under the image with 8px gap. Credit photographers when the image came from an open licence that needs it.
