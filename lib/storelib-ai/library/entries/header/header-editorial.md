**Shape**: a wordmark set large (40 to 96px) across the top, a thin rule, then a row of section links in small caps. On scroll, collapse to a compact bar with the small logo.

**Details that carry information**: today's date, the latest issue, a location. Only if real and maintained.

**Type**: wordmark in the display serif; links 12 to 14px caps, tracking 0.1em.

**Phones**: the wordmark shrinks to fit one line (clamp), section links move into a horizontally scrollable row or the menu drawer.

**Avoid**: a masthead that takes more than 30% of the first screen on phones; fake "Vol. 01" furniture.
