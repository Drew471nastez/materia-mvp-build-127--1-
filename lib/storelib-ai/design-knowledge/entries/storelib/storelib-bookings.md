**Before the booking step** people need: what the session is, duration, where (address or online), price or "free first call" as the creator set it, who runs it, cancellation terms. Put these next to the book button.

**The booking itself** happens in the creator's published booking form (Storelib Forms handles slots, time zones, double booking protection and optional payment). The capabilities report how many booking forms exist. Link or place that form; the page's job is to get people there with the right expectations.

**Never**: draw a fake calendar or time slot grid, show "3 spots left", or promise availability. If no booking form exists, use an enquiry form or contact link and say how the creator will reply.

**Schedules** (class timetables) are content the creator edits as blocks, or real event data; label them "Typical week" if they are not live availability.
