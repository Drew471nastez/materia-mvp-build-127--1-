**Template signature**: a small rounded badge above the heading ("New: AI powered"), a centred two line heading with a gradient word, a grey subheading, a primary and a ghost button side by side, a mockup with a glow, a logo strip.

**Evaluator checks**: in the first section, a pill or badge before the h1, two or more buttons together, centred text, a gradient or glow, and generic heading words.

**Fix**: decide what the visitor must see first for this business: the place (immersive photo), the product (product hero), the person (portrait led split), the statement (type led), the next date (event hero). Write a heading that only this business could use. One primary action; a secondary link only if it serves a different real need (for example "See the menu" next to "Book a table").
