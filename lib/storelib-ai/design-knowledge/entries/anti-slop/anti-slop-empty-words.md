**Flagged words and phrases**: unlock, elevate, seamless(ly), powerful, effortless(ly), supercharge, transform(ative), empower, streamline, revolutionise, game changer, next level, cutting edge, state of the art, world class, unleash, harness, leverage, robust, innovative, delve, embark, journey (metaphorical), tapestry, testament, in today's fast paced world, take it to the next level, look no further, whether you're X or Y.

**Why they fail**: they could describe any product, so they tell the visitor nothing, and readers have learned to recognise them as generated.

**Fix**: replace each with the specific thing. Ask "what does this actually do, for whom, how long, how many, made of what?"
- "Seamless booking" -> "Pick a time, pay, get a calendar invite."
- "Powerful presets" -> "14 presets for overcast street photography."
- "Elevate your wardrobe" -> "Heavyweight cotton basics, cut in Porto."

The evaluator counts them in visible text; one or two can be accidents, a pattern is an error.
