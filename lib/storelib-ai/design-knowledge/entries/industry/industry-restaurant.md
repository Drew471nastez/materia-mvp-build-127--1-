**Visitor**: usually on a phone, often nearby, asking "is it open, where is it, what is on the menu, can I book".

**Home**: a photo that shows the room or the food honestly -> today's hours and address in the first screen -> book a table or order (whichever exists) -> menu highlights or the full menu link -> the story (the people, the sourcing) -> location with map link -> footer with hours again.

**Menu**: real text, not a PDF or image (searchable, translatable, accessible). Sections, dish names, short descriptions, prices from what the creator gave, dietary markers explained once. Set it like a printed menu: dot leaders or right aligned prices, a narrow measure.

**Visual**: food photos in natural light, close; the room at service. Palette from the place (tiles, wood, signage). Typefaces with character: a bistro serif, a hand painted sign style used once, a utilitarian grotesk for a canteen.

**Don't**: autoplay video with sound, hours hidden in the footer only, stock food, feature cards ("Fresh ingredients", "Great service"), fake review stars.

**Storelib**: table booking as a booking form with real slots; gift cards and merch as products; events (wine nights) as events or products with dates.
