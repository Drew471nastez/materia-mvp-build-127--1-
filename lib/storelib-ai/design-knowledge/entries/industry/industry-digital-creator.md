**Visitor**: comparing a few similar products, wants to see inside before buying, worried about getting something thin.

**Product page structure**: name and the specific result ("A Notion system for running a 2 person studio") -> previews: real screenshots or page spreads, ideally a scrollable look inside -> what is included as a precise list (pages, files, formats, compatible apps and versions) -> who it is for and who it is not for -> price and buy -> delivery (instant download, link by email) -> refund policy if one exists -> FAQ.

**Home**: the creator (face and voice matter here), featured product, the rest of the catalogue from the store, free resource or newsletter if one exists.

**Visual**: let the product's own look set the site. A minimal planner shop can be paper white and precise; a Lightroom preset shop uses before and after sliders with the creator's own photos; a font or icon shop uses the asset itself as the hero.

**Honesty rules**: no invented download counts or "bestseller" labels unless store data supports them; no fake reviews; mockups must show the actual file, not a stock tablet with something else on it.

**Storelib**: digital products deliver files after checkout. Bundles are their own products with their own price.
