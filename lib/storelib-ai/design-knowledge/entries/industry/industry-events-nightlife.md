**Visitor**: deciding what to do this week, on a phone, often late. Date, place, price and ticket link must be readable in the first screen.

**Structure**: next event with date, venue and Get tickets -> upcoming dates as a list (date, name, venue, price, ticket link, sold out state from real data) -> line up or hosts -> gallery of past nights -> venue info (address, doors, age, accessibility) -> FAQ -> newsletter for announcements.

**Visual**: this is where loud is appropriate. Condensed display type at huge sizes, a marquee of dates, dark palettes with one hot accent, full bleed photos with grain. Still pick a concept: a jazz club and a techno night are not the same site.

**Lists over cards**: a schedule reads faster as rows than as a grid of cards. Each row is one link target to the event page.

**Motion**: rhythmic is fine (marquee, staggered entrances) but the date and button never move. Respect reduced motion.

**Honesty**: no "selling fast" without sales data, no fake countdowns, line up only as announced. Ticket prices and availability come from products or events.
