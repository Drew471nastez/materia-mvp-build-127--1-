**Visitor**: wants to feel safe before booking. Trust comes from the practitioner, clear practicalities and a tone that does not oversell.

**Structure**: hero with the practice and place (or online) -> what a session is like, in plain steps -> the practitioner -> offerings with duration and price from the store -> timetable or booking -> what to bring, accessibility, cancellation -> location and contact.

**Visual**: avoid the default sage green plus beige plus script font. Options: a mineral palette (stone, slate, chalk) with a geometric sans; a warm terracotta and ink palette with a book serif; a deep forest night palette for evening classes and sound baths. Photography of real rooms, hands and light; never stock lotus poses.

**Motion**: subtle. Slow fades (400 to 600ms), no bouncing, nothing that loops in the reading area. Breath based pacing is a cliché unless the brand is breathwork.

**Copy**: no medical claims, no "heal", "detox" or "cure" unless the creator's own regulated practice states it. Describe sensations and logistics: "75 minutes, mats provided, slow flow for beginners".

**Storelib**: classes and sessions are bookings with real slots; packs of classes are products; retreats are products or events with real dates. Never show available spots unless the booking data supplies them.
