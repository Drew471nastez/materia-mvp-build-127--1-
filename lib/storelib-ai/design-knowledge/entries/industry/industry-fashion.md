**Visitor**: browsing with their eyes. Photography quality and how product images sit together matter more than any copy.

**Structure**: a campaign image or short video with the current collection name -> the collection from the store in a grid that respects the photo ratio (4:5 or 3:4 for garments on body) -> a lookbook or editorial story -> brand line (who makes it, where) -> second collection or bestsellers from the store -> newsletter for drops.

**Product grid rules**: one consistent image ratio, second image on hover only if the product has one, name and price in small type under the image, no card borders or shadows around photos, sold out from the product's real availability. Two columns on phones, three or four on desktop.

**Product page**: large gallery (swipe on phones), name, price, variant pickers with real stock, size guide link if one exists, add to bag, materials and care from product fields. Do not invent fit notes.

**Directions**: streetwear is not only black and red; try a technical catalogue look (mono specs, numbered garments) or a sunlit editorial (warm whites, serif). Luxury see `industry-luxury-brand`.

**Never**: fake "only 2 left", made up reviews, discount banners the creator did not set, product prices typed into markup.
