**Signals**: two or three saturated colours on a light base, rounded radius (12 to 24px) or pill buttons, a rounded or quirky display face, illustrations or stickers made for the brand, springy hover states.

**System keeps it from chaos**: a fixed palette (background, ink, 2 to 3 fills), one radius value per component type, one shadow style, consistent icon stroke width.

**Motion**: springy (cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1)) at 200 to 350ms for small elements only; wiggles and bounces on hover are fine for one or two signature elements, not every card.

**Good fits**: kids brands, snacks, games, community clubs, creator merch, education for beginners.

**Don't**: use random emoji as icons, rainbow gradients by default, or comic sans style fonts for body text.
