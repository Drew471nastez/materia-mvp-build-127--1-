**Signals**: backgrounds `#0b0b0c` to `#151515` (not pure black for large areas), off white text `#ecebe8`, imagery with deep shadows, letterbox crops (2.39:1 or 21:9), slow reveals, grain or subtle vignette.

**Contrast**: body text at least 4.5:1 against the darkest part behind it. Grey text on black is the classic failure; `#8a8a8a` on `#0b0b0c` is about 5.8:1 and is the lowest comfortable muted tone.

**Light as accent**: one warm or cool accent (amber `#e0a050`, ice `#9fd3ff`) used for actions and small details. No neon glow halos.

**Video**: muted, `playsinline`, poster image, paused when off screen, a pause control. Do not autoplay on reduced motion.

**Motion**: 600 to 1000ms reveals, clip-path wipes, slow scale (1.06 to 1) on images. Text appears after the image, never before.

**Combines with**: editorial (film journal), luxury (fragrance, night collections), events.
